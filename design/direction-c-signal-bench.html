The mesh strip is a patch bay: cables only take colour when a peering is wrong.

10 · Honest states — failing watch & empty view

What changed: the two states most tools fake, made honest in bench vocabulary. A failing watch never renders a clean list: the panel keeps its last-good data behind a lit amber banner with the exact error, the last-good timestamp, and the retry countdown. An empty view says why it's empty, proves the operator (ESO) is healthy, and offers the two useful next moves — it is never mistaken for "loading" or "broken".
